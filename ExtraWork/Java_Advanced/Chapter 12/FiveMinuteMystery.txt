2. coffee objects to a stream of Strings, and then ordered that. Strings are naturally ordered

3. alphabetically, so when the coffee machine got the “first” of these results for Alex’s

5. If Alex wanted to order the coffees by strength, with the weakest (1 out of 5) first, she

6. needed to order the stream of coffees first, before mapping it to a String name,

7. afternoonCoffee = coffees.stream()
 .sorted()
 .map(Coffee::getName)
 .findFirst();
